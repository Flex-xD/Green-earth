Welcome to Green Earth!

Our planet is our home. Pollution, cutting down trees, and wasting water harm the environment. Small changes in our daily habits can make a big difference.

5 Simple Ways to Help the Environment

Start with these simple habits and make a positive impact.

🌳

Plant More Trees

Trees absorb carbon dioxide, provide shade, and create homes for birds and animals.

💧

Save Water

Turn off taps when not in use and avoid wasting clean drinking water.

♻️

Avoid Plastic Bags

Carry reusable cloth bags instead of single-use plastic bags while shopping.

💡

Save Electricity

Switch off lights, fans, and other electrical appliances when you don't need them.

🧹

Keep It Clean

Use dustbins, separate recyclable waste, and never litter on roads or in parks.
